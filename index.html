01 · FEATURED PRODUCT

CURAVIA — PATH TO CURE

A simpler way to navigate healthcare.

Curavia is a Telugu-first healthcare navigation platform built around the idea of a “Google Maps for Healthcare.” It is designed to help people discover and access relevant healthcare services, especially in Tier-2 cities.

Problem

Finding the right doctor, hospital or emergency resource can become confusing when every minute matters.

MVP

Healthcare navigation, emergency assistance guidance, nearby healthcare discovery and a Telugu AI Health Assistant.

Technology

Flutter, Firebase, AI-assisted workflows, location-based services and modern mobile UI.

Model

Doctor-booking and medicine-delivery commissions, healthcare partnerships and a premium tier later.
